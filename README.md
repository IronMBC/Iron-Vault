1. What Is Iron Vault?
# Iron Vault

Iron Vault is my long-term cybersecurity and compter science knowledge base.

It's purpose is to eliminate knowledge debt by documenting concepts until they can be understood from first principles, applied practically, and recalled without memorization.

It is both a learning system and a record of my technical growth

Its aim is for nothing but understanding as the reward
